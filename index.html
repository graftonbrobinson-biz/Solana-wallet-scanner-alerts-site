Solana Wallet Scanner Alerts

A personal-use tool for monitoring a small, hand-picked set of Solana wallet addresses.

What this is

Solana Wallet Scanner Alerts is a personal application, built and operated by its creator for their own use. It watches a short, manually curated list of Solana wallet addresses that the operator has chosen because of their historical on-chain trading activity. All of the activity it reads — token swaps, balances, trade history — is public information already recorded on the Solana blockchain.

When enough of those watched wallets buy the same token within a short window of each other (a "confluence" signal), or when they sell or fully exit a position they previously held, the application can send a text message alert. The alert is sent to exactly one phone number: the operator's own number.

Who this is for

This is not a public product, a subscription service, or a multi-user platform. There is no sign-up form, no account system, and no public list of subscribers. The sole recipient of any alert is the person who built and operates this tool, for their own personal, informational use.

What this is not

  • It is not a brokerage, exchange, or custodian — it never holds funds and never places trades.
  • It is not financial, investment, or trading advice. See the Terms & Conditions page for the full disclaimer.
  • It does not collect information from the public, and it is not open for anyone else to opt in to receive its alerts.

How alerts are delivered

Text message alerts are sent through Twilio, a third-party messaging provider. See the SMS Opt-In page for consent, frequency, and opt-out details, and the Privacy Policy page for what data is collected and how it is used.

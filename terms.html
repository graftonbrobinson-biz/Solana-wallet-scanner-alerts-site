Message types and frequency

Two kinds of automated notification may be sent: BUY alerts (coordinated buying detected across watched wallets) and SELL alerts (a sell or exit detected in those same wallets). Frequency varies with on-chain activity and will not exceed 25 messages per day. See the SMS Opt-In page for full consent and frequency details.
